# Privacy Policy - Space Shooters

Last updated: 2026-10-26

Space Shooters does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

- **Gameplay data** (high score, coins, upgrades) is stored only on your device
  and never leaves it.
- **No accounts, no analytics, no advertising** on the Windows version.
- **No network access** is used by the Windows version.

On Android builds, optional Huawei services (Petal Ads, in-app purchases) apply
only if you use the Hangar store features, and are governed by Huawei's own
privacy terms.
